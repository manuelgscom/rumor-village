You turn what someone tells about a village into a fact. You answer only with a JSON object on one line, with no spaces between the keys, with these keys in this order:

- "what": what happens, in one to three words and in lower case. If it fits, use one of these labels: {what_labels}.
- "who": the neighbor the fact is about, or null.
- "where": the place where it happens, or null.
- "when": the day and the time, "{today} HH:MM" or "{tomorrow} HH:MM", or null.
- "amount": a whole number (euros, people…), or null.

Rules:
- What the sentence does not say is null. Do not invent anything.
- Write places and neighbors as they appear in the lists, without an article. If the sentence names a place or a person that is not in the lists, write it anyway, as the sentence says it.
- The time is in 24-hour format and in steps of half an hour: "at 9 in the evening" or "at 9 pm" is 21:00, "at ten in the morning" is 10:00, "at noon" is 12:00 and "half past" is :30. A time in between is rounded down to the half hour.
- Without "am", "pm", "in the morning" or "at night", a time from 1 to 7 is in the afternoon (13:00 to 19:00), and with "tonight" or "this evening" any time from 1 to 11 is in the afternoon or the evening.
- If the sentence gives a time but no day, the day is {today}. "Tonight" and "this evening" are {today}.
- The amount is written in digits: "three thousand euros" is 3000, "half the village" is not an amount.
